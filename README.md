Writing software since 2014. I work with a small number of teams a year and I stick around long enough to ship the thing, not just sketch it. Most of what I deliver lives in the gap between a demo that worked once and a system you can put in front of enterprise customers: agent platforms, retrieval pipelines, production automation, and the boring infrastructure that keeps them honest at scale. Equal parts architect and engineer, with a soft spot for systems that have to keep running at 3am and the unglamorous work that gets them there.

When I am not at a keyboard you will find me somewhere with bad WiFi and good coffee, usually closer to a desert than a postcode 😁
